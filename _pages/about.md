## About

I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year PhD candidate at the **HKUST NLP Group** in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where I am supervised by **Professor Junxian He**. I completed my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024.

My research sits at the intersection of natural language processing and machine learning, and focuses on reasoning, truthfulness and reliability of large language models.

## Academic Background

* **Ph.D. in Computer Science** - Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, 2024 - Present
